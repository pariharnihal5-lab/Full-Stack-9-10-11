Steps to Cook Maggie

  1. Gas On
  2. Put Kadhai
  3. Put Oil
  4. Put Onion
  5. Put Tomato
  6. Put Salt
  7. Put Masala
  8. Put Water
  9. Put Maggie
  10. Boil for 2 minutes
  11. Turn off the gas
  12. Take off on a Bowl
  13. Enjoyy!!
