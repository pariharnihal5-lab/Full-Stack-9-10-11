HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information to make your visit straightforward.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the appointment form on this page to tell us how to reach you and which specialist you would like to see. For urgent care, please call our emergency line.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance information, a list of current medications, and any relevant test results or medical records.

Do you offer emergency care?

Our emergency response team is available around the clock. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency number immediately.

Can I choose a specific doctor?

Yes. Choose a doctor when you submit the appointment form, or use the doctor directory to find a specialist by name or department.
